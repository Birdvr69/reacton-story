# Story Ideas for Climate Change Story

## Main Story
Maya is 16, lives in Alaska. She notices glaciers melting. Makes choices about climate action.

## Scenes
- Scene 1: Maya sees old photos of bigger glaciers. Brother asks why ice melts. School has climate talk - go or skip?
- Scene 2A: At school talk, learns about changes, joins club or stays quiet
- Scene 2B: Skips talk, family dinner about warmer winters, talk to family or not
- Scene 3: Different paths based on choices
- Endings: 3 different outcomes based on what you choose

## Endings
- Good: Become climate scientist, family changes too
- Okay: Make small changes, teach others
- Bad: Ignore it, world gets worse

## Notes
- Keep it simple
- Choices matter
- Show real climate issues
